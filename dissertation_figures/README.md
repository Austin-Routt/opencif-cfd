# The dissertation's CFD figures

The figures of the dissertation that rest on the CFD of this repository, as the dissertation prints them (PDF, vector; PNG, 600 dpi). They were drawn on 9 November 2026 by the dissertation outline's figure scripts (named below, in the outline's `tools/`, with matplotlib 3.10.9) from the files of this repository, in the colors of the dissertation's Chapters 1 and 2. A figure changes only when its data change; `DISSERTATION_MAP.md` lists where the dissertation uses each one. The archive's own comparison figures, drawn by Python CFD's `cfd/src/figures.py`, are in `cfd/figures/`.
